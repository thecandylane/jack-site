[ref-radar](https://ref.lookit.space) is a catalog I keep so a coding agent can ask three things before it writes something new. Who already built this. Where do I start reading. Can I reuse the code.

This site and ref-radar share the bar at the top and the `Search>` box. They are separate hosts. Lookit, the search engine, has not shipped. ref-radar is the part that is live, at ref.lookit.space.

## What a ref is

A ref is one project, pinned to a commit. It has a why line, the files to open first, and a reuse verdict taken from the license: copy, copyleft, or study. The catalog is a file, [catalog.json](https://ref.lookit.space/catalog.json). Agents can also read [llms.txt](https://ref.lookit.space/llms.txt) or call the MCP server at `https://ref.lookit.space/mcp`. No account is required to read.

```
claude mcp add --transport http ref-radar https://ref.lookit.space/mcp
```

## What it counted

On 18 November 2026 the catalog file listed 4,208 refs. 4,167 were curated and 41 were discovered. Keyed use over the previous 30 days was 1. A keyed use is a logged-in `ref report`, not an anonymous search.

Over the seven days ending 18 November 2026, Cloudflare counted 164 requests to the ref-radar Worker and no errors. On the lookit.space zone, `/lane` had 94 eyeball requests and the homepage had 35. `/api/search` was not in the twenty heaviest paths. Reloads and bots are in those counts.

## What a miss is

A search from a logged-in key that finds nothing above the relevance floor is recorded as a miss. Anonymous searches are not. Each day, up to ten of those misses are asked again of GitHub, awesome lists, Hugging Face, arXiv, and the package registries. A repo that comes back from a miss is filed as a suggestion. It does not publish itself.

Keyed use moving off 1, and misses turning into refs, are the two counts I am using. Page views that week were small.
